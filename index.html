How the recommendations are made

Forecast demand = average weekly demand x the season's index for that product category. Safety stock = z x sqrt(lead time x demand variance + demand squared x lead-time variance). Reorder point = demand during lead time + safety stock. If stock plus orders in transit is below the reorder point, it is time to order, up to the reorder point plus 4 weeks of demand. Over 12 weeks of cover counts as overstock. In production, a time-series model (for example Prophet or gradient boosting) would replace the simple seasonal index, and real sales, stock and supplier data would replace the simulated numbers.
